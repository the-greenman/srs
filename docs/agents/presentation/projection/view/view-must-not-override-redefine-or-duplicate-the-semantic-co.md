> A View must not override, redefine, or duplicate the semantic content of any Field or Type it references.

Specification: SRS 2.0-draft, data model revision 11. Part of the [SRS specification index](/llms.txt). Up: [View](/agents/presentation/projection/view.md).

**Number**: 14

A `View` must not override, redefine, or duplicate the semantic content of any `Field` or `Type` it references. View-level `aiGuidance` is workflow framing; it does not redefine Field extraction semantics.
